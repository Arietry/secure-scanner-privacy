Questions

Do I need an account to make a code?

No. The generator is free and open — pick a type, fill it in, download. Accounts arrive with the paid plans.

Do free codes have a watermark?

Yes — codes made on the Free plan carry a small Secure Scanner mark beneath the code. It never covers the code itself, so it stays fully scannable. Pro removes it.

Will my codes keep working?

Codes you download are static and yours forever. Dynamic codes (editable destinations) are a Pro feature launching soon.

Where does scanning happen?

Scanning — with the safety check that flags dangerous links — lives in the Secure Scanner mobile app. This site is the generator.
